04 · The MLS

The open door most owners never get to use

A major advantage — at no extra cost to you

We list your property in the MLS

Beyond the consumer sites, we place your property in the MLS — the same system real-estate agents use every day. That opens your property to every agent in the market with a client looking to rent: a huge, often-overlooked source of qualified, agent-represented tenants.

To make agents eager to bring their clients, we pay the cooperating-broker (co-broke) referral fee from our side — and it's already included in your management package, at no additional cost to you.
